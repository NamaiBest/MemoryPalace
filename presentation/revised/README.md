# Memorypalace — HackMIT presentation

Open **Memorypalace-HackMIT.pptx** in desktop PowerPoint for embedded video playback. The PDF is a static visual backup.

- 14 slides, widescreen 16:9; designed at 1920 × 1080.
- Slide 2: click the film to play the revised 36-second introduction with sound.
- Slides 6–7: click to play the short baseline and four-window explanations.
- Slides 9 and 14: live demo links require the local application at http://localhost:3000/.
- Slides 10–12: Elastic, Meta, and Voloridge sponsor stories.
- Three native charts include editable workbook data. On slide 6, move the video aside to edit the chart beneath it.
- Speaker notes contain implementation details and sources. Twenty minutes is an onboarding target; current EEG baseline collection is 120 seconds.

Standalone copies of all three videos are retained in assets/. The original presentation is unchanged.
